About Burn Bright

Burn Bright is a collective space in the heart of Jacksonville for artists, makers, and dreamers. Our mission is to provide an inspiring environment where creatives can grow, collaborate, and thrive together.

Creative industries: Since age 5!

Preferred Medium: Oil & Mixed

Previous Chapters: Photography major 1995-2000, Fashion Design major 2003, Fashion label (NYC) 2005-2015, Historic building renovator 1999-present, serial entrepeneur since birth!

Background: Grew up with a southern rock musician father and a photographer mother; the creative community was always all around me. Fell in love with art and other artists my whole life. Education: Instituto Marangoni (London/UK) fashion design and illustration, some art classes here and there (Dustin Harewood is king)
