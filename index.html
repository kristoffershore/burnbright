House rules & requirements

A few basics to keep everyone safe and the space pristine.

Booking & changes

Reschedules free up to 24h before your slot; cancellations within 48h may incur a fee. Overtime billed in 30‑minute increments.

Respect the space

No open flame or haze without prior approval. Tape floors only with gaffer’s. Return gear to carts and leave spaces clean.

Insurance

COI required for productions over 15 people or if bringing 3rd‑party vendors. We can recommend providers.

By booking you agree to our studio agreement and liability waiver (provided at confirmation).

Frequently asked questions

If you don’t see your answer here, just ask—we’re friendly.

What gear is included?▼
Basic LED panels, stands, sandbags, extension cords, and V‑flats are included. Specialty gear (strobes, continuous kits, mics) is available as an add‑on.
Can I host an audience?▼
Yes—intimate audience events (up to venue capacity) are allowed with advance notice. Additional staffing and insurance may be required.
Do you offer student rates?▼
We offer 15% off for students with a valid ID Monday–Thursday, subject to availability.
Is there parking?▼
Free on‑site parking and street parking are available. Load‑in zone at the rear entrance.
