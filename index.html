About Burn Bright

Burn Bright is a collective space in the heart of Jacksonville for artists, makers, and dreamers. Our mission is to provide an inspiring environment where creatives can grow, collaborate, and thrive together.

Community

Shared Energy

Our tenants include painters, videographers, printers, and musicians who bring their work to life here every day.

Collaboration

Ideas flow freely between studios, whether we’re organizing a gallery or a photoshoot.

Space

Made for Making

Mark Zimmerman

Photography & video

Years in the Industry: I fell in love with photography in high school. When my college counselor asked me what I wanted to do in college, I had no idea what to say, so I just said, I like photography. 25-years later, art and photography is life. If industry equals life. The answer is, life!

Preferred Medium: The medium that best fits the concept, which for me, is most often photography or video.

Previous Chapters / Background: Art has been such a significant part of my life since I left home for college. Since I moved to Savannah in the early 2000’s, finding new opportunities to be a more engaged member of the art community has been my goal. Before that, I ran a jewelry business and made WWII replica leather pieces, but that was high school. Working in the service industry paid the bills and allowed me to make work, before I became an arts educator.
Born and raised in Louisville, KY. That is where it all began and I still consider Louisville to be my home.

Education: BFA in Photography from SCAD and MFA in Photography from the University of Florida.

Personal Life / Hobbies: I am always looking to learn more, in many subjects, but I spend most of my spare time researching art and new techniques. I spend much of my free time seeing live music.

Anything Else? Love collaboration and learning from others. I am always down for conversation and collaboration.

Stacy Streeter

Multidisciplinary artist

Years in the Industry: I have over twenty years of experience in the art world and art education.

Preferred Medium: Difficult to choose. Trained in photography and printmaking, my practice also includes sculpture, video, and installation. I’m drawn to drawing, collage, and monoprinting—I love working across mediums.

Previous Chapters / Background: After graduate school, I began teaching in higher education before transitioning to work in both public and private institutions for over fourteen years. I have always lived in Florida—Jacksonville is home. Growing up in different parts of the city has given me a strong sense of place, which continues to influence my work, particularly my interest in maps.

Education: I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Studio Arts from the University of Central Florida and a Master of Fine Arts from the University of Florida.

Personal Life / Hobbies: I love camping, traveling with my family, and going on cruises. The beach and anything outdoors are my happy place. I also enjoy concerts and festivals, and I’m always on the go—I don’t sit still for long.

Anything Else? I love to collaborate! I’m constantly imagining ambitious projects that call for shared energy—reach out if you’d like to create something together.

Website: www.stacystreeter.com
